Rules & Prizes

1. Participation & Deadlines

The Bracket Challenge is open to anyone. To participate, users must submit their bracket providing a unique Bracket Name and a valid E-mail Address. Submissions close exactly 1 minute before the tip-off of the very first game of each tournament bracket.

2. Scoring System

  • Progression by Round: Points double each round. You earn 1 point for each correct game prediction in the First Round, 2 points in the Second Round, 4 points in the Third Round, 8 points in the Fourth Round (and so on for subsequent rounds).
  • Simple Advancement: Points are awarded simply by correctly predicting the team that wins a given round/matchup, regardless of whether your projected opponent in that slot was different.
  • Exact Series Score Bonus: In series-based rounds, correctly predicting both the winning team and the exact series score in wins (e.g., 4-2) grants a flat +3 bonus points (independent of the round).

3. Prizes

Prizes are awarded based on the final leaderboard standings for each separate bracket:

  • 1st Place: 6 months subscription to the "Basketball Maestro" tier on The Eurostep's Patreon page.
  • 2nd Place: 4 months subscription to the "Basketball Maestro" tier on The Eurostep's Patreon page.
  • 3rd Place: 2 months subscription to the "Basketball Maestro" tier on The Eurostep's Patreon page.

4. Tie-Breakers & Standings

In the event of a tie in the final standings, all tied users will occupy the highest possible position according to the leaderboard. Specifically, prizes will not be scaled down; multiple winners at the same tier will each receive the corresponding prize (e.g., two 1st-place ties mean both win the 1st-place prize, and the next score tier qualifies for the 3rd-place prize, with potential ties there as well).

5. Fair Play & Regulations

  • One Bracket Per Email: Each participant is allowed to submit strictly 1 bracket per category (NBA, Euroleague, NCAA) per email address. Multiple brackets linked to the same email address will invalidate all submissions associated with that email.
  • Postponements & Rescheduling: In the event of match postponements, scoring will await the official rescheduling and completion of the fixture.
  • Unforeseen Circumstances: In the event of severe unforeseen circumstances or extraordinary events, the administrator reserves the full right to decide autonomously whether and how the competition will proceed.
In case of controversies or unconsidered situations, the decision of the administrator of The Eurostep is final.
